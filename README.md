# Through Their Eyes

An interactive data story about colour vision deficiency (CVD). You are assigned a type of colour vision, live a day as Alex in eight small decisions, then follow the evidence: who is affected, what real charts look like through each pair of eyes, and what design fixes work.

English only. Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript (ES5). No build step, no framework, no tracking.

> This is the **post-study** version, rebuilt for PacificVis 2027 (VisNotes and the Visual Data Storytelling Contest). The version used as the experimental arm of the CHI trial (Chinese default, completion code, `?study=1`) is kept unchanged under the git tag [`study-version-2026-11`](../../tree/study-version-2026-11).

## Run it

```bash
python -m http.server 8765
```

Open `http://localhost:8765/`. Opening `index.html` directly may work, but some browsers block features on `file://`.

## Story flow (top menu)

| Menu item | Screens | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Intro | `hero` | Letter-by-letter title, a drag-to-compare map (typical vision vs deuteranopia) |
| Prologue | `choose`, `brief`, 8 scenes, `sunset`, `flamingo`, `twist` | The day, the reveal, "what the videos skip" |
| The Numbers | `evidence` | Animated stat cards, a patterned prevalence chart, literature, method and sources |
| The Charts | `charts` | Scroll-driven: three real charts pinned while the pair of eyes changes; a random sample of 120 public charts (open data, frozen thresholds) summarised by chart kind |
| The People | `voices` | Composite voices from public interviews |
| The Fix | `lab`, `wins` | Accessibility lab, real-world before/after |
| Palette | `palette` | A four-act guided story (see, fix, add a cue, your turn): palette editor, four pairs of eyes, pattern as second cue, check your own chart. A small link opens the standalone tool |
| Epilogue | `done` | What you can do |

## Data and honesty notes

- The three featured charts come from a 30-chart trial set picked by hand. That set is **not a sample**.
- The headline numbers come from **120 charts drawn at random** from the 4,292 in Our World in Data's sitemap (seed 20261119), analysed once with the checker's thresholds frozen beforehand (`chart_audit/sampling_protocol.md`, `frozen_config.json`). 51 of 120 (43%, 95% interval 34 to 51%) have at least one flagged pair. The checker is not validated against people with CVD; one publisher only. The page says so.
- Prevalence figures: Birch 2012 (about 8% of European men, 0.4% of women, 4 to 6.5% of Chinese and Japanese men) and Gao 2023 (4.11% of men and 0.16% of women among 17,303 Chinese undergraduates; 52.3% of affected students knew before the college-entrance exam). Gao's numbers were read from the paper's text.
- The "voices" are composites paraphrased from public interviews, and are labelled as such.
- Charts use colour **and** pattern **and** printed values, so they read without colour.
- Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Not the same as the control site

The control article of the study has no counterpart for The Charts, Palette or the number corrections. This version is no longer information-matched to it.
